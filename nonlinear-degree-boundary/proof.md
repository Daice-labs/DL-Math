# An exact degree-four boundary for a nonlinear Boolean source

The static Nullstellensatz degree below charges the degree of a multiplier plus the degree of its original equation before Boolean reduction. The family theorem concerns the same source equations at odd primes. Source: v72, §6.8 and Supplements S59 and S68.

## Source equations and proof of the exact degree boundary

All variable values lie in GF(2); subscripts lie in GF(5). For a,c in GF(5), define the 25 source rows and extra row by

$$
Pₐ,c = x₀,ₐ x₁,ₐ₊c + x₂,ₐ₊₂c + x₃,ₐ₊₃c + x₄,ₐ₊₄c,
P★ = x₀,₀ x₁,₁ + x₂,₄ + x₃,₄ + x₄,₁ + 1.
$$

The source requires every P to vanish in the Boolean quotient x² = x. It is the p = 5 instance of the family proved in S68, so it is unsatisfiable. Independently, the retained 32 branch certificates set each of the five guard variables and give an explicit affine row combination equal to one. These are two routes to original-source inconsistency.

Every original row has degree two. Under the frozen convention that charges multiplier degree plus original-row degree before product reduction, a degree-at-most-three certificate can use only affine multipliers. Its entire possible span is therefore generated by the 26 rows Pᵢ and their 650 products xⱼPᵢ. The saved linear functional λ on multilinear monomials of degree at most three has

$$
λ(1) = 1,     λ(Pᵢ) = 0,     λ(xⱼPᵢ) = 0
$$

for all 26 rows and all 25 variables, including tail variables. Applying λ to any putative certificate Σᵢ AᵢPᵢ = 1 gives 0 = 1. This excludes every certificate in the stated language, not only those attempted during search. A separate saved identity expands to 308 original row–multiplier terms and reduces exactly to one; all its multiplier monomials have degree at most two. It supplies the matching degree-four upper bound.

The [finite certificates](certificates.json) give the original rows, right-hand sides, 114 nonzero dual coordinates, 308 terms of the upper identity, and 32 affine branch certificates. All variable and row indices are zero-based. Each row lists its two product variables followed by its linear variables. Bit $i$ of the right-hand-side integer is the constant in row $i$. Each term of the upper identity is a row index followed by its multiplier's variable indices.

## Original equations and complete family argument

Work over GF(2), with indices in GF(p). Let p be an odd prime for which Φₚ is irreducible over GF(2). Put gₐ = x₀,ₐ, hᵣ = x₁,ᵣ and zₜ(v) = xₜ,ᵥ for t other than zero and one. The original rows are

$$
Pₐ,c = gₐhₐ₊c + Σ_{t ≠ 0,1} zₜ(a + ct) = 0,
P★ = g₀h₁ + Σ_{t ≠ 0,1} zₜ(t²) + 1 = 0.
$$

Define G = Σₐgₐ, H = Σᵣhᵣ, Zₜ = Σᵥzₜ(v), L = ΣₜZₜ and Dc = Σₐgₐhₐ₊c. Summing actual rows at fixed a, fixed a+c, and fixed c gives

$$
gₐH = L,     Ghᵣ = L,     Dc = L.
$$

The first two use that multiplication by t and by 1−t permutes GF(p). Hence H(gₐ+g₀) = 0, G(hᵣ+h₀) = 0 and every Dc is equal. Also ΣcDc = GH. If H = 1, g is constant; it is either zero, or all-one and then h is all-one. If H = 0 and G = 1, h is constant and its odd-length sum forces it to be zero. These cases make the product matrix M(a,c) = gₐhₐ₊c constant.

It remains to treat G = H = 0. Oddness of p and equality of the Dc imply Dc = 0. In R = GF(2)[X]/(Xᵖ−1), the kernel J of evaluation at X=1 is, by the Chinese remainder decomposition, isomorphic to GF(2)[X]/(Φₚ). The factors X−1 and Φₚ are coprime because Φₚ(1)=1. By irreducibility the latter quotient is a field. Associate g with ΣₐgₐX^(−a) and h with ΣᵣhᵣXʳ. They lie in J and their product has coefficients Dc, all zero. Thus one factor is zero. The product matrix is again constant, now zero.

For any tail direction define Aₜ(v) by summing M(a,c) over the p pairs satisfying a+ct=v. Substitute the original affine rows into this sum. The t-direction contributes zₜ(v) p times, hence once in characteristic two; every other direction contributes its full sum. Therefore

$$
Aₜ(v) = zₜ(v) + L + Zₜ.
$$

Sum at v=t² over the p−2 tail directions. Both p and p−2 are odd, and ΣₜZₜ=L, so the two copies of L cancel. If the product matrix has constant value μ, every Aₜ equals μ and their sum equals μ. The extra output is consequently g₀h₁ + Σₜzₜ(t²) = μ+μ = 0, contradicting its prescribed value one. This proves original-source UNSAT without enumerating assignments.

The same identity shows each tail column is constant and proves that any row selecting one coordinate in each column has output zero. For the affine-only zero fibre, either g=0 or h=0 and the tail constants sum to zero, or g=h=all-one and the tail constants sum to one. Counting these disjoint cases gives (2^(p+1)−1)2^(p−3) + 2^(p−3) = 2^(2p−2). This cardinality argument is a paper corollary, not a separate Lean counting theorem. The p=7 witness in S68.1 remains a boundary outside the irreducibility class.

## Uniform source theorem and boundary witness

For each odd prime p with Phi_p irreducible over GF(2), the original source cannot produce the structured output with p squared zero affine-indexed entries and a final one. Actual source-row sums give guard, partner and slope relations. The cyclotomic quotient is a field; the even-parity convolution argument has no zero divisor. The resulting constant product and tail columns force the extra selected output to zero. Structured.lean proves this bridge from the original source, rather than taking a no-zero-divisor assumption as an unexplained interface premise. Its original_any_extra_zero endpoint permits an arbitrary selected coordinate in each column under the same hypotheses.

The separately initialized reviewer rebuilt Structured.lean and SevenWitness.lean without producer object files, checked the theorem through a separately written explicit signature, and audited the permitted axioms. Thirty-two uniform declarations use only the standard three Lean axioms; the two p = 7 witness declarations use only propext. The p = 7 assignment satisfies all 49 original zero-output rows and the extra output one, demonstrating failure if the condition is dropped. This proves necessity of retaining a condition for the stated extension, not an if-and-only-if classification of every prime. Infinitude of the irreducible-prime class is not established here. The reviewed paper characterization counts exactly 2^(2p-2) assignments in the zero fibre for that class; the fibre cardinality is not separately Lean-formalized.

## Certificate construction and its distinct cost

Fix a guard assignment γ in {0,1}ᵖ. The remaining original rows are affine and inconsistent, so there are constants λγ,ᵢ with Σᵢλγ,ᵢPᵢ(γ,h,z)=1. Define the Boolean selector δγ(g) as the product of gₛ for γₛ=1 and 1+gₛ otherwise. In the Boolean quotient δγgₛ=γₛδγ and Σγδγ=1. Multiplying each branch identity by δγ and summing yields

$$
Σᵢ Aᵢ(g)Pᵢ(g,h,z) = 1,     Aᵢ(g) = Σγ λγ,ᵢδγ(g).
$$

Every multiplier has degree at most p, so the charged original-row degree is at most p+2. There are at most (p²+1)2ᵖ collected coefficient monomials, and branch elimination followed by the Boolean transform costs 2ᵖ times a polynomial in p arithmetic operations. At p=5 this gives degree seven, which is weaker than the independent degree-four witness in S59. The source-UNSAT theorem, this exponential construction and the finite optimal-degree result must remain distinct.
