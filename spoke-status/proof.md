# A Boolean decision rule for the nonzero outer ring

## Family and statement

Take any connected simple cubic base graph with $b\ge4$ vertices and $q=3b/2$ edges. Subdivide each base edge, add a simple ring of length $q$, and attach one distinct outer vertex to each subdivision vertex by a spoke. Use the inherited signed mod-3 incidence equations with charges chosen to admit the constant-one flow, and the mixed five-physical-bit encoding of each flow edge. One port has two bits for the codes $00,01,10$, the opposite port three indicators. Edge clauses transport the same trit. Vertices use the full cubic truth table. Include an explicit physical nonzero clause on every two-bit ring port. A scanner can forbid simultaneous zero on consecutive marked ports in any permutation. Allow any number of additional signed clauses of width at most two on the Boolean predicates “this two-bit spoke is $00$”. Print each clause by the physical substitution below and distribution, simplifying repeated literals and tautologies first. Shuffle variable identifiers, clause order and literal order arbitrarily. The graph, gauge, marked positions, scan and overlay are unavailable as solver advice.

**Theorem.** SAT decision, recovery of a complete original-bit model on SAT, and construction of a clausal refutation on UNSAT take polynomial bit work in the physical input length $L$ across this family, independent of scan crossings. A new verifier can validate either result in polynomial bit work. The proof below establishes the full growing physical theorem on paper. Lean verifies the central status-image and lifting equivalence at every width in [StatusImage.lean](StatusImage.lean). Separate finite Lean claims verify the original physical twins.

## Exact image of the status bits

Write $x_i$ for ring values in cyclic order and $s_i$ for the spoke out of site $i$. The outer equation is
$$
s_i=1+x_{i-1}-x_i\pmod3 .
$$
The sum of the $s_i$ is $q$ in the field. To see explicitly why this suffices outside the ring, contract each subdivided oriented base edge back to one unknown edge value. The subdivision equation expresses its other half as an affine translation of the first using the specified spoke. In the remaining oriented base incidence equations the sum of the adjusted demands is zero: it is the original balanced charge after subtracting the already satisfied outer and subdivision equations. Equivalently, subtract the constant-one flow and the total injected exterior difference is $\sum_i(s_i-1)=0$. Assign off-tree base differences freely. From the leaves of any base spanning tree solve the last incident edge at each vertex. The last equation is the sum dependency. This produces all exterior lifts, with $3^{q-b+1}$ choices for each ring word. The edge bijections determine both physical ports uniquely.

Only $x_i=1,2$ remain. In this alphabet $z_i=[s_i=0]$ is precisely the ordered transition $1\to2$. Two adjacent $z_i$'s cannot both hold. Conversely every cyclic Boolean word with no consecutive ones lifts: set $x_i=1+z_i$. If $z_i=1$ then $x_{i-1}=1,x_i=2$. If $z_i=0$ then $x_i=1$. This proves the exact image at every ring edge, including the closing edge. Scan constraints on two spokes become $\neg z_i\lor\neg z_j$. A scan constraint with a ring endpoint is automatically satisfied. Signed overlay clauses retain their signs. Thus the conjunction of ring exclusions, scan exclusions and the signed overlay is a 2-CNF decision problem on $q$ variables. The original number of models cannot be obtained by treating each feasible status word as one equally weighted model. Even on the bare guarded ring the all-false word has two ring lifts; a single marked rise permits $q-1$ choices for the fall. Both receive the same additional exterior factor.

## Recovery from the original bits

1. Locate disjoint positive triples declaring one-hot words. None of the overlay substitutions introduces such a triple. Group rows with exactly one negative hot bit and two non-hot bits by hot word and the unordered pair of other IDs. The six signed transport blockers isolate each two-bit partner. They exclude all mismatched trit pairs. A three-port vertex has either a second hot literal or two literals for each other binary word. A status clause involves only two-bit words. Check the partition, all domain rows, all six transport entries and all 18 vertex blockers. A negative pair by itself is ambiguous because the positive-positive status overlay also introduces negative pairs. Every original clause must be accounted for. Exact duplicates can share one semantic constraint, and the final checker still reads the original occurrence list.
2. Form the physical incidence graph. Mark edges with an explicit nonzero binary-word clause. Peel degree-zero and degree-one vertices from the marked subgraph. Unary constraints on spokes can attach leaves to the ring. They cannot create an additional marked cycle in this family. Require one simple cyclic remainder, one distinct spoke at each site and consistent incidence signs. Recover a canonical cycle direction and start by physical identifiers.
3. Project the recovered linear laws onto these $2q$ physical ports. The tree route uses outside-column components, the direct route sparse elimination with the same marked columns. Both retain a triangular lifting schedule. Every proper row has support consisting of one spoke and its two neighboring ring words. The additional exterior row is the sum dependency, checked from the cancelling nonzero incidence weights and balanced charge. Equivalently, when exterior pivots are removed, the recovered row relation spans the redundancy between the surviving component rows, all with nonzero coefficients. For this subdivided family the exterior component touches all $q$ spokes; each outer component touches exactly three marked words. This identifies the redundant row also for the smallest admissible $q=6$. The tree pivots solve all interior columns except their free columns. The direct route verifies the same projection by row operations. Measure the four possible nonzero pairs in the physical gauge of each recovered equation. The zero pair must be unique and the two neighboring requested symbols at a shared ring port must differ. With reordering or swapping the two nonzero labels, the witness rule picks the symbol required at site $i$ by its incoming zero transition when $z_i$ holds and the opposite symbol otherwise. This is the physical version of $x_i=1+z_i$. Check all linear equations during lifting.
4. To compile the remaining source rows, use the two-bit physical representation. The atom $z$ expands to $\neg h\land\neg l$, and $\neg z$ to $h\lor l$. On each residual scope of at most two marked words check at most nine legal trit pairs, restricting a guarded ring word to its two nonzero codes. Check explicitly that truth depends only on the relevant spoke zero flags. Each forbidden Boolean pattern contributes its blocking clause. Keep its original row IDs. A three-word or non-status relation is refused by this recognizer. None of these steps depends on a generator index or on guessing a scan order.
5. In the implication graph put an arc from each negated literal to its partner in the clause. A mutual path between a literal and its complement yields a contradiction. Otherwise a reverse-topological choice of complementary strongly connected components gives a satisfying status word. The resulting linear-time Boolean stage is the classical 2-SAT algorithm. [Aspvall, Plass and Tarjan](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(79)90002-4)

## Lifting proofs to the source

Choose as the Boolean proof variable the **original** opposite-port hot indicator whose value is true exactly for the two-bit word $00$. The six physical transport rows determine it. Each overlay or scan implication used by a conflict path follows from its residual physical scope, the domain and transport on at most two edges, and any local ring guards. A ring-neighbor exclusion uses the two physical outer-vertex tables, two spokes, three ring words, their transports and the three guards. Together these involve at most 25 original bits. Enumerate a local resolution tree under the negation of the claimed clause if unit propagation alone cannot prove it. At a conflict add the clause negating the path assumptions by reverse unit propagation; two complementary children justify their parent. Emit the actual sequence of clause identifiers for each such LRAT step. The bound is constant per used clause, including failures of local unit propagation. For the two implication paths derive a contradiction under the positive pivot, then the empty clause using the reverse path. There are $O(q)$ distinct arcs in two simple paths. The negative twin uses eight distinct status clauses even though the paths may revisit clauses across the two directions. If a constant false constraint is present, use its local derivation of the empty clause directly. SAT instead emits a value for every declared physical bit. The fresh checker validates on the supplied original bytes, with no trust in recovered graphs, coordinates, producer, or verdict metadata alone.

## Complete growing cost

Physical parsing, sorting, binding, rejecting extraneous tables and recovering the guarded cycle are polynomial. There are $O(L)$ trit variables and physical linear equations. A conservative direct implementation with explicit equation provenance uses $O(L^3)$ field operations for elimination and $O(L^2)$ field entries. Dense row-normalization and tracing the pivots are also polynomial; allowing repeated linear scans and indexed-bit costs gives the whole producer a conservative $O((L+2)^6)$ bit-work and $O((L+2)^3)$ working-bit bound. The bound also covers building the Boolean graph and SCC, up to two shortest implication paths, reconstructing a full physical assignment, all constant-size local searches, writing and reading indexed hints and output. Width-independent constants include the possible search on 25 local original bits. A refutation contains $O(q)$ local constant-size derivations plus two implication-path closures and uses $O((q+1)\log(L+2))$ indexed bits on the stated encoding. A fresh deterministic verifier can replay each LRAT unit hint directly against its recorded clause and check the empty conclusion; the same conservative polynomial envelope covers replay, data authentication, storage, and scanning every row of a SAT assignment. This is a bit-model result. It does not extrapolate wall time or the resource limits of the installed Lean executable to all sizes.

On a refused source charge the complete failed screen, raw decision and proof/model export, fresh verification and readback. This method gives no polynomial bound for generic fallback. Fixture generation and the prior toolchain build are separate setup; measured per-instance construction starts from frozen physical bytes.

## How far this extends

The union of ring adjacency and spoke scan adjacency has maximum degree four. Greedy selection leaves at least $\lceil q/5\rceil$ mutually nonadjacent spokes. Assign these any Boolean values and put all other statuses false. The preceding construction gives a physical model of the guarded substrate and scanner. Consequently arbitrary binary-clause instances can be embedded verbatim on a growing set of genuinely independent choices. Allowing unrestricted **signed three-status** clauses on those same slots already permits a polynomial reduction from arbitrary 3-SAT. For an $m$-variable input choose a cubic prism with $q\ge5m$ and $q=O(m+1)$, select the slots greedily, and physically distribute each three-literal clause into at most eight constant-width clauses. On the square-array subfamily choose the next square with side divisible by three; the cubic prism then has $q/3$ rungs for all sufficiently large inputs. Any satisfying status assignment lifts and every physical satisfying assignment restricts back. Membership in NP follows by checking the original assignment. This last corollary explains why one successful fallback on three statuses offers no general polynomial extension. It concerns SAT decision. Neither this theorem nor its finite replay settles P versus NP.
